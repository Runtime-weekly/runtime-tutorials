RUNTIME xLLM-Loop compatibility study, 2026.

Custom PyTorch SDPA inference port and experiment scripts by RUNTIME.
Based on equations and inference schedules from the xLLM project:
https://github.com/ifm-ai/xllm-loop
Pinned source: df0ed1ea415b55f0a944a291f90da9d47c98b5d5

Paper: Towards Looped Models Done Right, Part II: Rethinking at Fixed Points
Benhao Huang, Chufan Shi, Junlin Chen, Shicheng Wen, Zhengzhong Liu,
Eric Xing, Xuezhe Ma; Institute of Foundation Models, USC and CMU.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06833v1

Modifications: standalone inference-only SDPA blocks, strict released checkpoint
loading, local paired cache/timing probes, offline result analysis and controls.
This is not the official fused backend, and its numerical equivalence is not
established. Model weights and tokenizer are downloaded separately and retain
their upstream Apache-2.0 license and notices.
